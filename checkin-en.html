House Notes

  1. No smoking indoors. The whole building is non-smoking! If you'd rather not walk down to the ground floor, you may take the stairs by our door up to the rooftop above our unit. If a neighbor reports it, though, the smoker pays any fine.
  2. Please close the main entrance behind you (it beeps nonstop if left open!).
  3. Please sort your trash. Put food scraps in the fridge. Toilet paper can be flushed.
  4. The water dispenser filters are replaced regularly, so it's safe to drink. For hot water, press the child lock first, then press Hot once the light is on. Hold it down for continuous flow. Careful, it's hot!
  5. If you'd like to use the kitchen, please let us know first. There's an NT$500 deposit, fully refunded if it's left clean. Backpacker-plan guests pay a fee to use the kitchen.
  6. Backpacker plan: after check-out, please put the pillowcases in the laundry basket.
  7. Bluetooth speaker: JY3087
  8. Guests with pets: please keep your furry friends out of the tatami room and off the bean bag. Thanks!

Pet Policy

  1. Pets are not allowed on the beds or in the tatami room, and may not use the cups, towels or blankets in the unit.
  2. Please clean up after your pet anywhere it goes. If it isn't cleaned up, a cleaning fee will be charged. Any damage to furniture must be paid for at full cost.
  3. To be fair to our neighbors, we can't host pets that are very anxious, noisy and hard to control, or aggressive. If a pet seriously disturbs the neighbors, we reserve the right to end your stay without a refund. Thank you for understanding.
  4. Please don't leave your pet alone in the room. If you must, keep it in a pet carrier.
  5. Dogs must be leashed whenever they're outside the room.
  6. Please make sure your pet is flea-treated and free of contagious diseases.

Thank you for following these pet rules!
